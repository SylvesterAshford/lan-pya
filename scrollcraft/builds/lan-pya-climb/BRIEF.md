# Lan Pya landing: The Climb

**Self-authored under explicit creative delegation.** The founder asked for the
landing page to be "more fun and scroble style and graphics" and, offered the
eight-question interview, answered "use your judgement" (twice). Every answer
below is an authored decision, sourced from DESIGN.md, PRODUCT copy in
`messages/*.json`, and the founder concept art in `public/design/`. No user
quotations are invented.

## The eight topics

1. **Vibe.** Night hike, first light, earned. References: the last hour of a
   pre-dawn climb up Mount Popa or Natma Taung; the painted night mountain in
   `public/design/lan-pya-landing-hero-concept.png`; the mission-map concept's
   numbered stops on a winding trail. *(Evidence: founder concept art. Assumption: the hiking references.)*
2. **Journey.** Trailhead at night with the existing headline, then three stops
   on the way up (Choose, Build, Prove), a dark quiet stretch, then the summit
   at sunrise where the call to action lives. *(Authored, from the product's own
   Choose / Build / Prove / Connect sequence.)*
3. **Energy.** Calm at the trailhead, steady through the stops, quietest just
   below the top, then the largest change on the page at the summit, then still.
4. **Feeling and the one moment.** See the curve below. The moment: the sun
   comes up the instant you reach the top.
5. **The thing no other site does.** Scrolling *is* the climb: two students walk
   up a trail that draws itself under them, and the whole landscape regrades
   from night to dawn as they arrive.
6. **Range.** Playful-illustrated, not premium-minimal. Flat vector landscape,
   the product's own navy, teal and amber. Still bound by DESIGN.md's honesty
   rules: no invented numbers, no hype, no emoji.
7. **One world or scenes?** One unbroken world. The product metaphor is a map
   and a climb; cutting between scenes would break it.
8. **Assets.** Founder mascots `public/art/traveller.webp` and
   `traveller-f.webp` (cut out, 17KB each). Everything else is drawn as SVG.
   No generation, no spend: no KIE key is set, and the audience pays for data.

## Feeling curve

| Stop | Feeling | What causes it |
|---|---|---|
| Trailhead | Recognition | Night, a long way to go, the headline names the advice-collecting they already do |
| Choose | Relief | The first flag; one honest starting point instead of ten opinions |
| Build | Momentum | The trail drawing itself under them as they climb; a mission, real work |
| Prove | Trust | The flag turns teal with a check; a person reviewed it |
| Below the summit | Anticipation | **Authored silence.** No copy at all for a stretch, darkest sky, only the climb |
| Summit | **Awe, then relief** | The sun breaks over the peak and the whole landscape regrades from night to dawn |
| Close | Resolve | The page stops moving; one line, one action, at the top of the mountain |

## The peak

> "you scroll up a mountain in the dark and the sun comes up right as you get to the top"

Lives at the summit. It gets the largest share of scroll, the silence before it,
and the page's only colour change.

## Tell-someone sentence

It's the site where **you climb a mountain at night by scrolling, and the sun
rises when you reach the top.**

## Authored silence

Scroll progress roughly 0.70 to 0.79 carries no copy on purpose. Dead-scroll
reports in that band are expected.
